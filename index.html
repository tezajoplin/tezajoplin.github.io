Operations

Before marketing, there was this.

My first kind of growth had nothing to do with Google. It was shrimp ponds in Pontianak: 151 hectares, 14 workers, and harvests that had to survive a boat trip to Jakarta. I built the team routines, tracked costs, and figured out better ways to feed, harvest, and ship. These days, I oversee the business as an owner and still handle procurement and buyers.

Then came six months running a villa in Uluwatu. Three staff, international guests, and everything from room checks to a water pump that needed looking after. In 2025, I started JH Laundry. Monthly volume grew from 500–600 kg to 3–4 tons; an on-site team now runs the day-to-day while I manage remotely. Different businesses, same habit: get close to the problem, build a system, keep making it better.

About

I didn't plan to end up in marketing.

Started out managing shrimp ponds in a small village. Early mornings, waking up before everyone else, coordinating harvests, dealing with buyers all the way in Jakarta. Not exactly a typical background for someone who ended up obsessing over SEO. But here we are!

Then Bali happened. Felt like I needed a change, so I packed up and took a villa manager job there. That's also where my marketing journey quietly started: work by day, student by night.

Turned out I was pretty into the marketing side. Spent a couple of years learning digital marketing almost every day. SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads before officially running SEO for votelino.id.

Still figuring things out. The map just keeps getting bigger, and that's exactly how I like it.
