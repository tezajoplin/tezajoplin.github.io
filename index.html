Honestly? I didn't plan to end up in marketing.

I spent years managing aquaculture operations in Pontianak. Think early mornings, shrimp ponds, harvest logistics, and handling shipments all the way to buyers in Jakarta.

So I moved to Bali. Managed a villa during the day. Then at night, I'd join online classes for digital marketing. Most nights ended with a group meeting or just me gaming to decompress.

Turns out I was pretty into it. Right now I'm running SEO for votelino.id and looking to make my next move to Kuala Lumpur.
